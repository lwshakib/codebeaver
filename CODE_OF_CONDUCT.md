# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

CodeBeaver is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone. We are dedicated to creating a community where all participants feel valued, safe, and empowered to contribute their best work.

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces associated with the CodeBeaver project, including but not limited to:

- GitHub repositories (issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Official project websites
- Social media channels
- Official events and meetups
- In-person and virtual conferences or workshops
- Team communications and collaboration spaces

## Our Standards

We expect all participants to uphold these standards:

### ✅ Be Respectful

- Treat everyone with dignity and respect
- Acknowledge and celebrate differences in backgrounds, perspectives, and experiences
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Respect others' privacy and personal boundaries
- Use inclusive language that welcomes all genders, ethnicities, orientations, and abilities

### ✅ Be Inclusive

- Welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels
- Help others learn and grow
- Provide mentorship and support to newcomers
- Recognize the value of diverse perspectives and experiences
- Work to make the community accessible to everyone

### ✅ Be Collaborative

- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy towards other community members
- Accept responsibility and apologize when you make a mistake
- Focus on criticism of ideas, not personal attacks
- Lift others up and celebrate their contributions

### ✅ Be Professional

- Keep discussions focused and on-topic
- Maintain a professional tone in all communications
- Acknowledge and respect intellectual property
- Provide helpful and constructive feedback
- Be open to feedback about your own behavior

### ✅ Be Patient and Considerate

- Remember we're all volunteers or have limited time
- Help clarify things when confusion arises
- Ask questions in a genuine effort to understand others' positions
- Acknowledge that English may not be everyone's first language
- Be patient with newcomers learning community norms

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered unacceptable and will not be tolerated:

### 🚫 Harassment and Discrimination

- Harassment based on, but not limited to: race, ethnicity, national origin, color, caste, sex, sexual orientation, gender, gender identity and expression, age, disability, health condition, political or religious affiliation
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Unwelcome requests for sexual favors
- Inappropriate comments about someone's body, appearance, or personal characteristics

### 🚫 Hateful and Violent Speech

- Slurs, insults, or dehumanizing language
- Threats of violence or harm
- Wishes of death or serious injury upon someone
- Hate speech based on identity or belief
- Incitement to violence or harm

### 🚫 Deceptive and Malicious Behavior

- Deliberate misrepresentation or lying
- Publishing private information ("doxxing") without consent
- Malicious or fraudulent contributions
- Attempts to sabotage projects or community
- Unauthorized use of others' work or identity

### 🚫 Exclusionary Behavior

- Gatekeeping or creating unnecessary barriers to participation
- Dismissing or minimizing others' concerns or experiences
- Excluding people based on identity or experience level
- Creating exclusive subcommunities that undermine inclusion

### 🚫 Unprofessional Conduct

- Excessive profanity or vulgar language
- Spam or repetitive messaging
- Deliberate derailing of discussions
- Off-topic promotions or advertising
- Bad faith arguments or trolling

### 🚫 Violation of Privacy and Intellectual Property

- Sharing others' private information without consent
- Using code or content without proper attribution
- Copyright infringement
- Unauthorized collection or use of personal data

## Enforcement

### Reporting Guidelines

If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it:

1. **In-person or immediate concern**: Address the person directly if you feel safe doing so
2. **Report to maintainers**: Email `conduct@codebeaver.dev` with:
   - Description of the incident
   - Who was involved (usernames/names if comfortable)
   - When and where it occurred
   - Screenshots or logs if available
   - Any previous related incidents
   - What resolution you're seeking

3. **Anonymity**: You can report anonymously through a third party or form

**We will:**

- Take all reports seriously
- Maintain confidentiality of reporters
- Not retaliate against those who report in good faith
- Investigate thoroughly
- Respond within 7 days with initial findings

### Consequences

Enforcement will be proportional to the offense and might include:

1. **Warning**: First minor infractions may result in a private warning
2. **Comment Removal**: Removal of unwelcome or violating comments
3. **Temporary Mute**: Temporary ban from communications (24 hours to 30 days)
4. **Temporary Ban**: Temporary suspension from community (30 days to 6 months)
5. **Permanent Ban**: Permanent removal from community

**Appeals**: Community members may appeal moderation decisions to `appeals@codebeaver.dev` within 30 days with new information or context.

## Examples

### ✅ Good Examples

```
"I disagree with this approach. Here's an alternative that might work better because..."
"I'm new to this project. Could someone help me understand how X works?"
"I made a mistake in my PR. Let me revise that based on your feedback."
"Your perspective on this is really valuable. I hadn't considered that angle."
"I'd like to help! How can I contribute to this issue?"
```

### 🚫 Bad Examples

```
"That's a stupid idea. Only an idiot would suggest that."
"People like you don't belong in tech."
"I'm posting your real name and address because I disagree with your PR."
"You're just [slur] and don't deserve a place here."
"Your code is garbage and you should feel bad."
```

## Scope and Accountability

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- **All contributors**: Developers, maintainers, users, and community members
- **All spaces**: Both online and offline community interactions
- **All roles**: Whether you're a first-time contributor or a long-time maintainer

**No one is exempt.** This includes:

- Project maintainers
- Core team members
- Project founders
- Corporate sponsors

Maintainers are held to a higher standard and will be held accountable for upholding these standards.

## Supporting Our Community

Everyone plays a role in maintaining a positive community:

1. **Be an ally**: Support and advocate for people from underrepresented groups
2. **Speak up**: Gently correct others if they violate the Code of Conduct
3. **Mentor**: Help newcomers learn and feel welcome
4. **Lead by example**: Model the behavior we want to see
5. **Report concerns**: Help maintain our safe community by reporting violations
